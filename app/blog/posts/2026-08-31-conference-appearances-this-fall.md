description: A surprisingly busy fall of workshops, talks, conferences, and volunteering from New York to Toronto.

I don't travel to conferences as much as I used to for work now that I'm not in a DevRel role anymore. So I figured I'd have a nice, sedate fall and enjoy a few last kayak trips on the Ottawa River.

Boy, was I wrong.

My September and October have filled up with a number of appearances.

On September 8–9, I'll be helping out with [Sanity's](https://www.sanity.io/) second-ever developer conference, [Everything NYC](https://everything.sanity.io/). The 8th will be a hands-on workshop with customers, helping them solve real business problems with Sanity. The 9th will be a day of inspiring sessions, but I'd be lying if I didn't say I was most excited about the conference's artist in residence, [Shohei Fujimoto](https://sfjmt.org/), who'll close the show with a live art installation.

Then, on September 17 at our local web dev meetup, [Forward JS](https://forwardjs.com/), I'll give my new talk, *Sampling the Web: What Hip Hop and View Source Have in Common*, a test run. I'll attempt to link the sampling done by early hip hop culture to how we used to learn web development by doing View Source. I love getting feedback from our local tech community before taking a talk on the road.

Less than a week later, I'm off to Winnipeg, Manitoba for [Prairie Dev Con](https://www.prairiedevcon.com/) on September 21–22. [D'Arcy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/darcylussier/) does a fantastic job putting on this show every year. At the conference, I'll be giving my *No Frameworks for Old Devs* talk where I'll show folks how to build an RSS Reader without a heavy weight JavaScript framework.

Finally, I'll get a bit of a break before heading out to Portugal for Sanity's yearly all-staff. Once I get back, I'll be volunteering at [Ottawa OWASP Day](https://owasp.org/www-chapter-ottawa/), a one-day cybersecurity event on October 17 at the University of Ottawa STEM Complex.

Then, to wrap up a couple of months of heavier-than-usual travel, I'll head to Toronto for the [Web Unleashed](https://fitc.ca/event/webu26_ip/) conference on October 27–28, where I'll be giving the now battle-tested *Sampling the Web: What Hip Hop and View Source Have in Common* talk.

Thinking about all this travel just makes me feel tired. I can't imagine how 2023 me used to do this much travel every month.

If you're going to be at any of these events this fall, let me know so we can grab a coffee.
